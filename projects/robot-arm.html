Anti-Slosh Liquid Transport

Moving an open container of liquid quickly without spilling it. On a 5+1 axis robotic arm I combined jerk-limited (S-curve) trajectories, ZVD input shaping tuned to the liquid's sloshing mode, and active gimbal tilt compensation. The motion was simulated in MATLAB and then executed on a Kinetix 6000 servo rack.

Compared over the same move, a trapezoidal profile (1.55 s) peaks at roughly 26 mm of wall slosh and an S-curve alone (1.75 s) at about 20 mm. With ZVD shaping and tilt compensation (2.04 s), the slosh stays under 1 mm for the whole move and the liquid is still when the arm stops.

3-DOF Robot Arm

A 3-DOF articulated arm in an RRR (revolute-revolute-revolute) configuration, designed for NEMA17 stepper motors. I built a digital twin in Simulink to validate the kinematics and tune a cascade control scheme with feedforward compensation before committing to hardware. Simulation is complete; the physical build is still waiting on budget.

Design

Kinematic design of the RRR arm: link lengths, joint layout, and workspace analysis.

Simulation

Forward and inverse kinematics simulated to validate reach and motion before committing to hardware.

Construction Planned

Physical construction using a NEMA17 stepper on each joint, waiting on budget.
